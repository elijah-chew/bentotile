How it works

Not another link-in-bio.

Choose your tiles

Start with hobbies, projects, skills, or a simple intro tile. Each one its own compartment, ready to fill.

Make it yours

Drag and drop until the layout feels right. Leave a little between tiles so each piece can stand on its own.

One link. Your world.

Publish a polished page and share it with universities, mentors, and friends. One link, arranged with care.
